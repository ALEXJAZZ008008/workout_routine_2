{\fontsize{9.6}{11.2}\selectfont\bfseries PROGRESSION \& RECOVERY}\par\vspace{0.65mm}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
{\fontsize{8.10}{9.8}\selectfont
\textbf{Reps:} Add one total rep per successful session, regardless of range: 3 sets 8/8/8 $\to$ 9/8/8 $\to$ 9/9/8; 2 sets 10/10 $\to$ 11/10 $\to$ 11/11.\par\vspace{0.45mm}
\textbf{Weight:} Increase to the next load only when every set reaches the range ceiling, then restart at the minimum. If the new load is too heavy, log actual reps and build back into range.\par\vspace{0.45mm}
\textbf{Effort:} Around 1 RIR; final set may reach 0--1 RIR. Technique and safe execution take priority.
}
\end{minipage}\hfill%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
{\fontsize{8.10}{9.8}\selectfont
\textbf{Rest:} Only while your partner works; no timed breaks. Do not add rest solely to hit a target; take extra time if safety requires it.\par\vspace{0.45mm}
\textbf{Progress:} Better control, technique or range (including fuller partials) counts. Off days and isolated stalls are normal; repeat missed targets.\par\vspace{0.45mm}
\textbf{Recovery:} If at least half of movements stagnate or regress across three consecutive sessions, skip the next scheduled session, then resume.
}
\end{minipage}
