\textbf{PROGRESSION \& RECOVERY}\par\vspace{0.6mm}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{Reps:} Add one total rep per successful session, regardless of range (3 sets: 8/8/8, 9/8/8, 9/9/8; 2 sets: 10/10, 11/10, 11/11). Repeat missed targets.\par
\textbf{Weight:} Increase only when every set reaches the range maximum; restart at its minimum. If too heavy, record actual reps and rebuild.\par
\textbf{Effort:} Aim for about 1 RIR; final set may reach 0--1 RIR. Prioritise technique.
\end{minipage}\hfill\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{Rest:} Rest only while your partner works. Do not add breaks to reach a target; pause if required for safety.\par
\textbf{Progress:} Better technique, control or range (including fuller partials) counts. Off days and isolated stalls are normal.\par
\textbf{Recovery:} If at least half of movements stall or regress for three consecutive sessions, skip the next session, then resume.
\end{minipage}
